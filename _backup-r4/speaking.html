THE SPEAKER

A VOICE SHAPED BY SERVICE, NOT A STAGE PERSONA.

Mike Schindler is a US Navy veteran, author of three books, and co-executive producer of It's VUCA. As a decision architect, he has impacted more than 7,500 leaders navigating the moments when the old answers no longer work.

He speaks about what he practices: clarity before decisions. His sessions give audiences a working sequence — clarity, decision, direction, action — they can apply to the transitions in front of them, whether that's a changing role, a changing organization, or a changing mission.
